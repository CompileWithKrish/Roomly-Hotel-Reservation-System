About This Room

Designed for uncompromising comfort, the Deluxe King Suite (Room 305) combines quiet luxury with purposeful tech. Situated high in the quieter East Wing, this 520-square-foot sanctuary features triple-glazed acoustic soundproof windows that shield you from city noise while presenting uninterrupted views of the metropolitan skyline.

Sink into an authentic California King mattress topped with tailor-made 400-thread-count Egyptian cotton linens, dual micro-gel down pillows, and automated blackout shades. Whether traveling for corporate leadership or a serene weekend retreat, enjoy a dedicated ergonomic workstation with dual USB-C rapid charging points and integrated room climate synchronization.
